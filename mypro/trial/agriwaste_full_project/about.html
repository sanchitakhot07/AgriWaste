Our mission & vision

About Us

At AgriWaste Marketplace, our mission is to reduce agricultural waste burning and dumping by connecting farmers with buyers who can repurpose crop residues as valuable raw materials. By doing so, we promote sustainable livelihoods, support renewable resource use, and minimize environmental impacts.

Why This Matters

  • Cleaner Air: Reduces air pollution caused by burning crop residues.
  • Farmer Income: Creates additional revenue streams for farmers.
  • Sustainability: Encourages a circular economy through reuse of agricultural waste.

Our Impact

  • Turning crop residues into compost improves soil health.
  • Producing biogas from agriwaste generates energy for local communities, reducing reliance on non-renewable sources.

At AgriWaste Marketplace, we’re committed to building a greener, more sustainable future—one crop residue at a time.
