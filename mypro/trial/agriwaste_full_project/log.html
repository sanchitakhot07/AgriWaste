Welcome

AgriWaste Marketplace is a digital platform that connects farmers with industries. Crop residues and agricultural by-products from farmers are used as raw materials by industries. This helps reduce waste burning and pollution, provides farmers with additional income, and promotes sustainability and a circular economy.

Our platform ensures that farmers gain extra income by selling waste, while industries benefit from low-cost, sustainable resources. This creates a win-win solution that promotes circular economy principles and supports environmental sustainability.

Beyond trade, AgriWaste Marketplace also spreads awareness about eco-friendly practices, including composting, biogas generation, and recycling. By bridging the gap between waste generators and waste users, we aim to build a cleaner, greener future for both rural communities and industries.
